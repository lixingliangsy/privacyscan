### What is GDPR Art. 6 lawfulness of processing?
- slug: `what-is-gdpr-art-6-lawfulness`
- type: Definitional · FAQPage
- query: GDPR Article 6 lawfulness explained
- body: Art. 6 lists lawful bases (consent, contract, legitimate interests, etc.). Each processing purpose needs a documented basis. Consent (Art. 7) must be freely given, specific, and withdrawable.
- refs: https://gdpr-info.eu/art-6-gdpr/, https://gdpr-info.eu/art-7-gdpr/, https://gdpr-info.eu/art-32-gdpr/

### GDPR Art. 32 security of processing — basics for product teams
- slug: `gdpr-art-32-security-basics`
- type: Definitional
- query: GDPR Article 32 security of processing
- body: Art. 32 requires appropriate technical and organisational measures. For SaaS: access control, encryption in transit, retention limits, and vendor diligence — not a checkbox guarantee.
